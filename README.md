Dr. Hindatu Salisu Abubakar, the Librarian: Katsina State Institute of Technology and Management.
Dr. Hindatu Salisu Abubakar is a distinguished academic and an administrator. She earned her Bachelor Degree, Master Degree and a PhD in Library and Information Science all
from Bayero University, Kano. 
Dr. Hindatu  began her academic career at Bayero University in 2012, ascending to the rank of Senior Lecturer in 2023. In same 2023,  She was appointed  Librarian of Katsina State Institute of Technology and Management, Katsina. In December 2024, She became the Ag. Rector and also the Director Academics of the Institute coupled with her position as a librarian.
Dr. Hindatu  is a member of several professional bodies, including the Nigerian Library Association, a Certified Librarian of Nigeria and also a Fellow in the Institute of Policy Management and Development and a fellow of the Cooperate Administrative Institute.
My Area of Interest are Information Science, children’s Library Services, Readership promotions, library advocacy and community services
